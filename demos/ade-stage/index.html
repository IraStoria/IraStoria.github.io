ADE ─ 四鋼琴環繞

ADE 樂章寫給四台鋼琴：前、後、左、右各一台，觀眾站在中間。四台同時演奏各自的內容，合起來才是一首曲子；站的位置不同，聽到的重點、音量與方位也不同。

這個展示以桌面當場地、滑鼠當你的位置，需要在電腦版桌面上進行。

ADE — Four-Piano Surround

An ADE movement written for four pianos — front, back, left and right — with the audience in the middle. All four play their own material at once and only together form the piece; where you stand changes what you hear, how loud and from where.

The desktop is the hall and the pointer is you, so this one runs on the desktop shell.
