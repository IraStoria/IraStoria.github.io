About

IraStoria — composer and game audio designer. My long-running interest is how music responds to interaction: not pasting music onto a screen, but letting the music itself follow the player's rhythm in a structured way.

I am currently working on a rhythm game and a home-grown DAW / notation tool. This site collects works, hands-on concept demos, and scattered notes.
